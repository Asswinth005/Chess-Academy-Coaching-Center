The Process

How Our Tournaments Work

From registration to trophy — a clear, professional experience every time.

01

Register & Confirm

Register online through our student portal or contact the front desk. Entry fees are collected at registration, and you'll receive a confirmation email with all event details.

02

Pre-Tournament Prep

For FIDE-rated events, students receive dedicated preparation sessions covering opening lines, opponent tendencies, and time management strategy. Coaches are available for individual consultations.

03

Play & Compete

Tournaments are run by certified arbiters with proper pairing systems. Live boards broadcast top games, and refreshments are provided throughout the event. Coaches are on-site for support.

04

Post-Game Analysis

Every game is reviewed with your coach within a week. We identify key moments, missed opportunities, and patterns to work on — turning every tournament into a learning experience.

Rewards

Prizes & Recognition

We reward performance, effort, and improvement across all levels.

Cash Prizes

Top finishers in major events share a guaranteed prize fund, distributed by placement and rating category.

Trophies & Medals

Every in-house event awards custom trophies and medals for top 3 overall and top performers in each rating band.

Rating Improvement

Special awards for students with the largest ELO gain over the season — celebrating growth, not just victory.

Travel Support

Top students receive subsidized travel and coaching support for national and international FIDE events.
