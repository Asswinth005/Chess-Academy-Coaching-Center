Common Questions

Program FAQ

How do I know which program is right for my level?

We offer a free assessment session for all new students. A coach will evaluate your current skill level, playing style, and goals, then recommend the best-fit program. If you already have a FIDE or national rating, that typically determines your placement.

Can I switch programs mid-year?

Absolutely. Our programs are designed to be flexible. When your coach observes consistent improvement and readiness, you can transition to the next level. Many students move from Beginner to Intermediate within 6–12 months.

Are there age restrictions for the programs?

We welcome students from age 5 through adult. Our Beginner program has dedicated groups for juniors (ages 5–12) and adults. Advanced and Private programs are open to all ages based on skill level.

Do you offer online training options?

Yes. All programs are available both in-person at our academy and online via our interactive platform. Online students receive the same curriculum, game analysis, and access to the student portal. Private lessons are especially popular online.

What is included in the student portal?

The student portal gives you 24/7 access to lesson notes, puzzle assignments, your game database with coach annotations, progress reports, tournament schedules, and training resources. It's included with every paid program.
