Founded On 64 Squares

From a Historic Manhattan Club to a World-Class Training Institution

In 2012, Grandmaster Alexander Vance and a cadre of FIDE titled trainers established the Chess Academy & Coaching Center with a singular vision: to dismantle the antiquated reliance on rote opening memorization and instead cultivate an intuitive, profound understanding of the board.

We recognized that whether an eight-year-old takes their first diagonal leap with a bishop or an experienced candidate master prepares for the World Open, the essence of chess is psychological resilience, spatial awareness, and decisive problem solving.

Today, our academy operates from a state-of-the-art facility in Manhattan and connects over 1,200 active trainees globally through interactive digital sensory labs, real-time Grandmaster feedback, and structured masterclass cohorts.

The Grandmaster Creed

  • Positional Foresight: Understanding the subtle motives that dictate squares moves before they materialize.
  • Analytical Discipline: Rigorous candidate-move calculation without guessing or impulse.
  • Tournament Composure: Calm under clock pressure and unwavering sportsmanship against any adversary.
  • Lifelong Mastery: Treating every loss as a private lesson and every triumph with humility.

The 4 Pillars of Academy Coaching

Every training track at our academy is anchored in four interconnected competencies designed to turn intuitive impulses into grandmaster-level precision.

1. Tactical Calculation

Deep pattern recognition, forced variations, and eliminating blind spots with structured tactical drills.

10,000+ Curated Motifs

2. Positional Strategy

Mastering pawn structures, outposts, prophylaxis, weak squares, and classical plans from Karpov and Capablanca.

Structure Blueprinting

3. Endgame Precision

Theoretical rook endgames, king-and-pawn technique, fortress dismantling, and flawless conversion of advantages.

Zero Margin for Error

4. Tournament Mindset

Psychological resilience, time management under sudden death, preparation routines, and post-game analytical debriefs.

Our Trajectory

Milestones in Chess Pedagogy

From a humble gathering of passionate tacticians to a recognized pinnacle of international chess coaching.

2012 • The Foundation

The Manhattan Strategy Club

Started with just 12 prodigy students in a classical Manhattan hall, focusing entirely on deep endgame fundamentals and master analysis.

2016 • National Sweep

First Triple National Titles

Three academy students captured top honors at the US National K-12 Championship, establishing our academy as a premier competitive powerhouse.
