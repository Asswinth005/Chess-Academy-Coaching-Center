Before You Reach Out

Contact FAQ

Quick answers to the most common questions we receive.

How quickly will I get a response?

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 4 business hours. Messages sent outside of operating hours (Mon–Sat, 9AM–8PM EST) will be answered the next business morning. WhatsApp inquiries typically get a response within 1–2 hours during operating hours.

Is the trial lesson really free?

Yes. Every new student is entitled to one complimentary 45-minute trial lesson with a coach matched to their level. This includes a skill assessment and a personalized recommendation for which program fits best. No payment information is required to book.

Can I visit the academy before enrolling?

Absolutely. We welcome walk-in tours during operating hours — no appointment necessary. You can observe an ongoing class, meet the coaches, and see the training facilities. For a more personalized tour, we recommend calling ahead at +1 (800) 555-0199.

Do you offer online lessons for remote students?

Yes. All our coaches offer online lessons via our interactive platform with shared boards, video, and live analysis. Online students receive the same curriculum, game analysis, and student portal access as in-person students. Private lessons are especially popular with remote learners.

What ages do you accept?

We welcome students from age 5 through adult. Our Beginner program has dedicated junior groups (ages 5–12) and adult groups. Intermediate, Advanced, and Private programs are open to all ages based on skill level. Many of our adult students started as complete beginners.
